**TL;DR:** The Anytime Reusable Menstrual Cup comes in two sizes: Small holds roughly 15 ml and Large holds approximately 28–30 ml. Your choice mainly comes down to your flow and what feels comfortable for you. There is no single right answer, and it is okay to try one size and adjust later.

## What size menstrual cup should you choose?

If you are new to menstrual cups, the size question can feel like the hardest part. Should you start small? Does a heavier flow mean you need a larger cup? What if you choose wrong?

Those are fair questions. The useful way to think about it is not "which size is best?" but "which size could work for me?" This cup comes in two sizes, and the right one depends on your flow, your comfort and how you plan to use it.

## How does your flow affect the size you choose?

Flow is personal and can change from day to day or cycle to cycle. A cup with a larger capacity may mean you can go longer between emptying on heavier days, while a smaller capacity cup may suit lighter days or those who prefer to change more often.

The cup can be used for up to eight hours before removing and cleaning. If your flow is heavier, you may find you need to empty it more frequently than that, regardless of size. The eight-hour limit is a maximum, not a target.

There is no rule that says you must choose one size for your whole cycle. Some people keep both sizes on hand and use the Small on lighter days and the Large on heavier ones. That is a practical approach if you are unsure.

## Does your body shape or history matter?

General information about menstrual cups often mentions factors like whether you have given birth vaginally or your pelvic floor tone. This product's information does not make specific recommendations based on those factors, so we are not going to invent them here.

What we can say is that comfort matters. A cup that feels comfortable and stays in place is more likely to work well for you than one that does not, regardless of its stated capacity. If you are between sizes and unsure, you might consider starting with the size that matches your flow needs and adjusting if it does not feel right.

## What if you are not sure which size to pick?

If you are genuinely unsure, here are a few practical ways to decide:

- **Think about your heaviest days.** If you usually need to change pads or tampons frequently, the larger capacity of the Large may be worth considering.
- **Consider your comfort with changing.** Some people prefer to empty a cup less often; others prefer to change more frequently. Neither is wrong.
- **Remember that size is not permanent.** You can try one size and, if it does not suit you, try the other. Many people end up owning both.

It is also worth reading the instruction manual that comes with the cup before first use. It contains guidance specific to this product.

## What are the trade-offs of a reusable cup?

A reusable menstrual cup is not for everyone, and it is honest to say so. Here are some common trade-offs:

- **Learning curve.** Insertion and removal can take practice. Some people get the hang of it quickly; others need a few cycles.
- **Cleaning.** The cup needs to be cleaned between uses, following the instructions supplied with it.
- **Comfort.** Not everyone finds a cup comfortable, and that is okay. Pads, tampons, period underwear and menstrual discs are all valid alternatives.
- **Upfront cost.** A reusable cup is a single purchase rather than a repeat one, but the initial outlay may be higher than a box of disposables. We are deliberately not putting a dollar figure on that, as prices vary.

On the other hand, a reusable cup can reduce repeat purchases and ongoing period waste. It is reusable for a typical lifespan of 5 to 10 years with proper care, according to the product information. It can also be used for swimming and exercise when correctly inserted.

## How do you know if you have chosen the right size?

There is no perfect test, but a few signs may suggest you have found a good fit:

- The cup feels comfortable and you are not constantly aware of it.
- It stays in place during your normal activities.
- You can go about your day without unexpected leaks within the eight-hour use window.

If you experience persistent discomfort or leaking, it is worth reviewing the instruction manual and considering whether the other size might suit you better. If problems continue, speak to a qualified health professional.
