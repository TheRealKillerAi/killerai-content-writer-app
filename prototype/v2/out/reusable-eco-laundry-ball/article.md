## Where does the ball go?

The ball goes in the drum with your clothes. It won't fit in the detergent drawer, so there's no need to try.

## How much laundry does one ball handle?

One ball is designed for a 4 kg wash. If your load is heavier than that, use two balls. This keeps the wash simple without guessing.

## What about detergent and other products?

The ball uses natural minerals to clean, so you don't add detergent, fragrance or fabric softener. If you're used to a particular detergent, you may notice the difference in scent or feel — that's expected with a mineral-based approach.
