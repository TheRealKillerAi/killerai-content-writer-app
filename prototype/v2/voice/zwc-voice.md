# Voice guide: Zero Waste Co (derived from the 13 Oct menstrual cup post)
- Second person, calm and plain-spoken. Short paragraphs. Australian English.
- Opens with the reader's real questions, in a short run of questions, then reframes: the useful question is rarely "is it better?" but "could it work for you?".
- Balanced and honest: says when a product is not for everyone, names trade-offs (learning curve, cleaning, comfort), mentions alternatives fairly. Never pushy.
- Careful with claims: "can reduce", "may", "according to the instructions for this exact product". Is open about what it does not know or cannot promise, without commenting on its own writing process.
- Health topics: "Stop if you experience pain", follow the supplied instructions, see a qualified health professional. Ends with a general-information-only note.
- Structure: TL;DR first, question-style H2s, short lists for trade-offs, a gentle "ready to explore" product section near the end, then a Sources list.
- Ends with a reassuring, low-pressure note (reader stays in control). No hype words, no emoji in body copy.

Do NOT reuse any wording from earlier posts. The voice is a set of traits, not a script.
