A reusable menstrual cup is a small silicone cup you insert to collect period flow instead of absorbing it, then empty, clean and reuse. This Anytime cup comes in Small (roughly 15 ml) or Large (approximately 28–30 ml), is made from medical-grade silicone, and includes a storage pouch and instruction manual.

If you're weighing up whether to switch from pads or tampons, or you're simply looking for a reusable menstrual cup in Australia, this guide covers what the product actually offers, how the two sizes compare, and what to think about before your first cycle.
