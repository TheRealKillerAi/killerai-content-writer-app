## How to use it

The routine is deliberately simple:

1. **One ball per 4 kg wash.** Match the ball to the load size as stated in the product information.
2. **Add your clothes as normal** and run your usual cycle.
3. **Recharge in sunlight for a few hours each month** with regular use. This is part of keeping the ball working, so it's worth putting a reminder in your phone.
4. **Refill when needed** rather than buying a new ball.

There's no measuring, no cap to rinse, no bottle to lug home from the supermarket. For anyone who finds the measuring step the most annoying part of laundry, that's the appeal.
